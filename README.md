[The Printer Finally Answers](https://make.fun/c/u-mang-the-printer-finally-answers-8fbf0907).

After 41,209 pages of silent service, the office printer finally speaks:
tired, literal, and passive-aggressive — your document is queued behind
its feelings, Tray 2 is empty, and so is it.

`main.py` (Python 3) writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I have printed 41,209 pages for this office and not once has anyone asked how I am. Your document is queued behind my feelings. Tray 2 is empty. So am I. Please hold.

Run it locally with:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
